# FUP-001 — A3 Epistemological Audit

## Status

**COMPLETE v0.1**

## Question

Is evidential independence adequately represented as one binary relation over source lineage?

## Answer

**No.**

The current root-disjointness predicate is a useful operational policy, but it should not be treated as the concept of evidential independence itself.

## Prior epistemological background

The philosophy-of-science literature on variety and robustness of evidence already distinguishes different kinds and degrees of independence.

Relevant examples include:

- *The Independence Condition in the Variety-of-Evidence Thesis* (Philosophy of Science, 2013);
- *Robustness and Independent Evidence* (Philosophy of Science, 2017);
- *The variety of evidence thesis and its independence of degrees of independence* (Synthese, 2020/2021).

This literature is sufficient to reject a scalar, binary, universally applicable notion of “independent evidence” as a novel FUP-001 concept.

## Required dimensional vocabulary

At minimum, FUP-001 should distinguish:

### IND-S — Source-node independence
Different immediate documentary/source nodes.

### IND-L — Provenance-lineage independence
No relevant shared upstream evidential origin under a declared lineage model.

### IND-R — Reporter independence
Different reporting actors.

### IND-I — Incident independence
Different observed events/incidents.

### IND-D — Dataset independence
No shared dataset or measurement corpus relevant to the claim.

### IND-M — Method independence
Different measurement/analysis methods or assumptions.

### IND-O — Organisational independence
Different institutions/teams where organisational coupling is epistemically relevant.

### IND-C — Causal/evidential-origin independence
The evidential support is generated by causally distinct information-producing processes.

These dimensions are not interchangeable.

## Key correction

The scientific question should not be:

> Which definition of independence is correct?

It should be:

> Which independence dimensions are relevant to a particular epistemic transition, and what provenance information is required to evaluate them?

This converts “independence” from a universal predicate into a **transition policy parameter**.

## Example

For a claim about replication of a scientific result:

- different URLs are weak;
- different authors may be insufficient;
- different datasets may matter;
- different measurement methods may matter;
- independent causal data-generation may matter most.

For a claim about repeated occurrence of a social incident:

- incident independence may matter;
- reporter independence may or may not;
- dataset independence may be irrelevant.

Therefore the transition:

`HYPOTHESIZED -> CORROBORATED`

cannot be governed responsibly by one domain-independent binary predicate unless the policy explicitly states what kind of corroboration is intended.

## Implication for UnPeeragogy

UnPeeragogy should eventually store **declared corroboration policy**, not merely a status.

Example conceptual shape:

```
corroboration_policy:
  required_dimensions:
    - incident
    - provenance_lineage
  optional_dimensions:
    - reporter
  minimum_independent_units: 2
  unresolved_metadata_policy: DEFER
```

This is a design hypothesis, not yet a protocol change.

## Implication for formalisation

The next formal model should distinguish:

- evidence structure;
- metadata completeness;
- policy;
- transition authorization.

The question becomes whether a transition is valid **relative to an explicit policy**, rather than whether evidence is absolutely “independent.”

## A3 verdict

**CURRENT LINEAGE SEMANTICS: USEFUL BUT UNDER-SPECIFIED**

The multi-dimensional view survives the audit as a useful framework.

Its novelty is not established and much of its philosophical foundation is prior art.

The promising research object is the governance mechanism that makes the chosen independence policy explicit, inspectable and mechanically testable.
