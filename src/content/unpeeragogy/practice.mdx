### The Assumption

Patterns must be practiced, not just read. Practice is the bridge between theoretical knowledge and real-world application. Without practice, peeragogical groups risk becoming discussion clubs rather than action-oriented communities.

### Operational Reality

Everyone wants to be a peer. Nobody wants to be the person who actually does the work.

The vault records show knowledge-hoarding, decision-stagnation, and authority-attrition — the three failure modes of groups that talk about practice but never practice. Not because they do not value action. Because practice requires someone to own the outcome. And owning the outcome means accepting that failure is personal, not collective.

GitLab's stewardship handbook works because it documents who owns what. Drupal's governance transition took years because practice without ownership scaffolding produces the failure modes the vault records. Wikipedia's deletion debates show that even when people practice, without clear authority, practice becomes procedural combat.

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if everyone wants to be a peer — but nobody wants to be the person who actually does the work — and practice doesn't practice itself?"*

### Empirical Evidence

**Resilience Cases (Confirming):**
- [GitLab Stewardship Handbook](https://about.gitlab.com/handbook/company/stewardship/) — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction in open-core projects. Governance scaffolding enables practice by clarifying ownership.

**Fracture Cases (Complicating):**
- [Drupal Governance](https://www.drupal.org/governance) — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work. Practice without ownership scaffolding produces knowledge-hoarding and authority-attrition.
- [Wikipedia Deletion Debates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Articles_for_deletion) — Consensus processes produce de facto hierarchies through participation inequality. Even in communities that practice, without clear authority, practice becomes procedural combat.

### Operational Heuristics

**Positive Boundary:** Practice works when governance boundaries and responsibility assignment mechanisms are documented before practice begins — explicit structural scaffolding enables practice by clarifying who owns what.

**Negative Boundary:** Do not expect practice to emerge organically from enthusiasm and pattern awareness — this produces knowledge-hoarding and decision-stagnation because no one is authorized to make mistakes individually.
