### Il Dogma

Patterns must be practiced, not just read. Practice is the bridge between theoretical knowledge and real-world application. Without practice, peeragogical groups risk becoming discussion clubs rather than action-oriented communities.

### La Realtà

Everyone wants to be a peer. Nobody wants to be the person who actually does the work.

The vault records show knowledge-hoarding, decision-stagnation, and authority-attrition — the three failure modes of groups that talk about practice but never practice. Not because they don't value action. Because practice requires someone to own the outcome. And owning the outcome means accepting that failure is personal, not collective.

GitLab's stewardship handbook works because it documents who owns what. Drupal's governance transition took years because practice without ownership scaffolding produces the failure modes the vault records. Wikipedia's deletion debates show that even when people practice, without clear authority, practice becomes procedural combat.

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction in open-core projects. GitLab shows that governance scaffolding enables practice by clarifying ownership.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**drupal.org** — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work. Drupal demonstrates that practice without ownership scaffolding produces knowledge-hoarding and authority-attrition.

**en.wikipedia.org** — Wikipedia's consensus processes produce de facto hierarchies through participation inequality. Wikipedia shows that even in communities that practice, without clear authority, practice becomes procedural combat.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** La pratica funziona quando i confini di governance e i meccanismi di assegnazione delle responsabilità sono documentati prima che la pratica inizi. GitLab mostra che lo scaffolding strutturale esplicito permette la pratica perché chiarisce chi possiede cosa.

**Regola Negativa:** Non aspettarsi che la pratica emerga organicamente dall'entusiasmo e dalla consapevolezza dei pattern. I vault incidents mostrano che questo produce knowledge-hoarding e decision-stagnation perché nessuno è autorizzato a commettere errori individualmente.
