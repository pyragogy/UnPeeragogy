### Il Dogma

Groups should eliminate waste by identifying and removing unnecessary complexity, redundant processes, and accumulated mistakes. Simplification is inherently beneficial and groups can recognize and act on opportunities to streamline their operations.

### La Realtà

Telling people to "just simplify" is like telling them to "just be happy" — the advice assumes the problem is motivation, not structure.

Every group accumulates complexity. The question isn't whether to reduce — it's who has the authority to decide what counts as waste. GitLab's stewardship handbook documents explicit boundaries that enable reduction without contestation. Wikipedia's Good Article process uses transparent criteria that make reduction decisions reviewable. But Drupal's governance transition and Apache's meritocracy model show that reduction without clear decision-making authority produces contestation, not simplification.

The pattern assumes waste is objectively identifiable. It's not. One person's waste is another person's essential infrastructure. Reduction is a political act disguised as housekeeping.

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction in open-core projects. GitLab shows that explicit boundaries enable reduction decisions without contestation.

**en.wikipedia.org** — Peer assessment with transparent criteria and independent reviewers creates sustainable quality tiers. Wikipedia demonstrates that transparent criteria make reduction decisions reviewable and legitimate.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**drupal.org** — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work. Drupal shows that reduction without clear authority produces contestation, not simplification.

**apache.org** — Apache Software Foundation's governance model demonstrates that meritocracy-based systems make reduction decisions without broad consensus, creating tension between efficiency and inclusion.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** La riduzione funziona quando criteri trasparenti e processi di review indipendenti sono stabiliti prima di identificare gli sprechi. GitLab e Wikipedia mostrano che confini espliciti e criteri trasparenti rendono la riduzione reviewable e legittima.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare questo pattern senza framework di governance espliciti per l'autorità decisionale. Drupal e Apache mostrano che i tentativi di riduzione senza autorità chiara producono contestazione, non semplificazione.
