"Reduce complexity in the peeragogical project." "Find places where you can simplify."

The Reduce pattern: make things simpler. Remove what's unnecessary.

**Why it matters:** Reduce is the pattern that groups never apply. Because reducing means removing. And removing means admitting that something was unnecessary. That something you invested in was a waste. Groups don't admit waste easily.

The Handbook has grown through addition. Version after version, patterns are added. No one proposes removing a pattern that didn't work. Reduce is a pattern in the Handbook. It's never been applied to the Handbook. Irony.

**What to do instead:** Reduce is the most difficult pattern because it requires honesty. "This pattern didn't work. Let's remove it." "This section is redundant. Let's merge it." Reduce is about having the courage to cut. Groups that can't cut grow in size but not in quality. They become museums of their own history. Not tools for the present.

> **Perturbator:** *"Reduce: make it simpler. Good idea. When did you last simplify your group? Remove a meeting, a process, a role? Never. Because simplifying means admitting something was unnecessary. And groups don't admit mistakes. They accumulate them. Reduce is a pattern everyone loves in theory. No one applies it in practice."*
