### Il Dogma

Future direction statements for the Peeragogy Handbook — outlining planned updates, community growth targets, and resource allocation — presented as transparent roadmap and collective ambition.

### La Realtà

"Next steps" is what people write when they don't know what to do next.

GitLab's stewardship handbook shows that explicit governance boundaries produce sustainable direction — not "next steps" documents. Fedora FESCo demonstrates that elected roles with defined scope enable transparent roadmap decisions. But the Peeragogy Project's handbook direction statements and open future design bibliography show that direction documents without governance produce direction statements that are too vague to follow or contest.

The pattern isn't wrong. It's premature. The direction isn't the problem — the missing governance that would make direction meaningful is the problem.

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries reduces friction. GitLab shows that governance boundaries produce sustainable direction more effectively than direction documents.

**docs.fedoraproject.org** — Fedora Engineering Steering Committee with elected roles and defined scope demonstrates that transparent roadmap decisions require governance, not direction documents.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**about.gitlab.com** — GitLab's stewardship documentation shows that direction emerges from governance, not from direction statements. External direction documents without internal governance produce no direction.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** La direzione futura funziona quando i confini di governance sono documentati esplicitamente e i ruoli di autorità sono definiti prima della pianificazione strategica. Fedora e GitLab mostrano che i ruoli tecnici eletti con scope definito permettono decisioni di roadmap trasparenti.

**Regola Negativa:** Non fare affidamento su documenti di "next steps" come meccanismo di governance. Il pattern non è sbagliato — è prematuro. La direzione non è il problema — la governance mancante che renderebbe la direzione significativa è il problema.
