### Synthesis

These cases suggest that peer governance works not by eliminating uncertainty but by making it visible early. The truncated email address becomes a feature, not a bug — it signals where the real boundaries lie between stated intention and available capacity.

Reliable long-term outcomes emerge from transparent policies and defined roles, but the reliability comes from acknowledging what remains untested. A syllabus that documents its own governance gaps may serve learners better than one that promises seamless coordination it cannot deliver.

The question hangs: whether peer learning succeeds by planning better or by planning more honestly about what cannot be planned.
