### The Assumption

The syllabus presents a structured learning path with defined modules, readings, and activities, implying that clear documentation and transparent planning enable effective peer learning outcomes.

### Operational Reality

A syllabus without governance is just a wishlist with dates.

The vault incidents show decision-stagnation, knowledge-hoarding, and authority-attrition in the syllabus implementation — the same three failure modes that appear every time a plan is treated as self-executing. GitLab's stewardship handbook and Debian's stable release process show that transparent policies produce results when paired with authority mechanisms. Wikipedia's ArbCom shows that even the most comprehensive documentation cannot substitute for governance.

Syllabus 2021 is another plan in a long line of plans. The pattern does not fail because the modules are wrong. It fails because planning without execution authority produces the same outcome as no planning at all.

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if a syllabus without governance is just a wishlist with dates — and planning without execution authority produces the same outcome as no planning at all?"*

### Empirical Evidence

**Resilience Cases (Confirming):**
- [GitLab Stewardship Handbook](https://about.gitlab.com/handbook/company/stewardship/) — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries reduces friction. Transparent policies paired with authority mechanisms produce results.
- [Debian Stable Release Process](https://www.debian.org/doc/debian-policy/) — Defined release managers and explicit policies demonstrate that documentation paired with governance produces sustainable outcomes.

**Fracture Cases (Complicating):**
- [Wikipedia Arbitration Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbitration_Committee_(Wikimedia)) — Comprehensive documentation cannot substitute for governance when decisions are contested.

### Operational Heuristics

**Positive Boundary:** The syllabus works when documentation is paired with explicit governance boundaries and defined roles — transparent policies paired with authority mechanisms produce sustainable outcomes.

**Negative Boundary:** Do not rely on syllabus documentation alone without governance structures — clear documentation without authority mechanisms leads to decision-stagnation, knowledge-hoarding, and authority-attrition.

### Epistemic Synthesis

Syllabus 2021 is another plan in a long line of plans. The pattern does not fail because the modules are wrong. It fails because planning without execution authority produces the same outcome as no planning at all. Three identical failure modes — decision-stagnation, knowledge-hoarding, authority-attrition — appear every time a plan is treated as self-executing.
