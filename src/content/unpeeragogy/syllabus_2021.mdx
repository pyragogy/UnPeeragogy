### Il Dogma

The syllabus presents a structured learning path with defined modules, readings, and activities, implying that clear documentation and transparent planning enable effective peer learning outcomes.

### La Realtà

A syllabus without governance is just a wishlist with dates.

The vault incidents show decision-stagnation, knowledge-hoarding, and authority-attrition in the syllabus implementation — the same three failure modes that appear every time a plan is treated as self-executing. GitLab's stewardship handbook and Debian's stable release process show that transparent policies produce results when paired with authority mechanisms. Wikipedia's ArbCom shows that even the most comprehensive documentation cannot substitute for governance.

Syllabus 2021 is another plan in a long line of plans. The pattern doesn't fail because the modules are wrong. It fails because planning without execution authority produces the same outcome as no planning at all.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if a syllabus without governance is just a wishlist with dates — and planning without execution authority produces the same outcome as no planning at all?"*

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Il syllabus funziona quando la documentazione è abbinata a confini di governance espliciti e ruoli definiti. GitLab e Debian mostrano che politiche trasparenti abbinate a meccanismi di autorità producono risultati sostenibili.

**Regola Negativa:** Non fare affidamento sulla sola documentazione del syllabus senza strutture di governance. I syllabus_2021 incidents mostrano che la documentazione chiara senza meccanismi di autorità porta a decision-stagnation.
