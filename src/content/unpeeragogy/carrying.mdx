### Il Dogma

The carrying pattern describes how peer groups handle members who declare inability to contribute fully. The theory suggests that groups can absorb and redistribute the load when some members become fragile, maintaining collective function through mutual support. Recognizing and communicating limits is the first step toward sustainable peer production.

### La Realtà

The Wikipedia administrator who burned out after three years told me she quit the day she realized she was doing other people's work for free. Not editing articles — that was the joy. The endless mediation between editors who couldn't agree on a comma placement. The cleanup of vandalism that happened faster than she could revert it. The careful diplomacy required to keep productive contributors from leaving over personality conflicts.

She wasn't carrying the project's intellectual load. She was carrying its emotional overhead — the friction that accumulates when humans try to coordinate without hierarchy. Not the creative struggle of building something together. The exhausting work of preventing that something from falling apart.

Every peer production system discovers this eventually. Someone ends up carrying what everyone else won't.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if the people who talk most about distributed leadership are the ones least willing to distribute the actual work?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**wiki.osmfoundation.org** — Formal dispute resolution bodies with binding authority and published reasoning resolve peer production conflicts that informal consensus cannot. OpenStreetMap's Data Working Group shows that someone must carry the authority to make binding decisions.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**docs.kernel.org** — External pressure can force governance changes in peer communities that would not arise from internal deliberation alone. The Linux kernel Code of Conflict shows that peer communities cannot resolve chronic carrying problems through informal support alone — external escalation mechanisms are required.

**w3.org** — Choosing lighter governance models for standards development trades authority for speed, creating different failure modes. WebAssembly Community Group demonstrates that lighter governance prevents one kind of carrying burden (bureaucracy) while creating another (consensus fatigue).

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Applica questo pattern quando esistono organi formali di risoluzione dispute come backstop del supporto informale. OpenStreetMap dimostra che il ragionamento pubblicato e le decisioni vincolanti riducono il carico emotivo su chi media.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare questo pattern quando la fragilità colpisce il nucleo decisionale o diventa cronica senza meccanismi di escalation formali. Il kernel Linux dimostra che le comunità peer non possono risolvere problemi cronici di carrying solo attraverso il supporto informale.

**Stato:** PROVVISORIO

L'assunzione che gruppi possano assorbire e redistribuire il carico attraverso supporto reciproco è contraddetta dall'evidenza. OpenStreetMap mostra che serve autorità formale per risolvere ciò che il consenso informale non può. Il kernel Linux mostra che la pressione esterna è necessaria quando i meccanismi interni falliscono. Carrying qualcuno suona nobile finché non sei tu quello portato verso l'uscita.
