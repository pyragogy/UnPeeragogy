### Il Dogma

Shared governance emerges when communities establish transparent decision-making processes and distribute authority across multiple stakeholders. The theory assumes this combination — transparency plus distribution — produces resilient collective intelligence. The 5PH1NX experiment supposedly demonstrates this in practice: students creating collaborative riddles across media platforms as evidence of emergent governance.

### La Realtà

Preston documents his 5PH1NX experiment — students creating collaborative riddles across media platforms — as if the acronym itself weren't already performing the problem. The handbook presents this as assessment innovation, but anyone who has watched students game rubrics knows better. Not theoretical engagement. Not authentic collaboration. Not learning that transfers beyond the assignment's artificial boundaries.

The Wikipedia ArbCom case exposes what happens when governance boundaries blur into endless process. Committee members spent months debating content moderation policies while actual editors abandoned contested articles. The community fractured along ideological lines — some demanding stricter oversight, others defending editorial autonomy. The result wasn't resolution but exhaustion.

Governance without limits becomes governance without function.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if the riddle isn't meant to be solved — but to reveal who stops trying first?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction in open-core projects. GitLab's stewardship handbook defines where corporate strategy ends and community autonomy begins. The boundaries were explicit before conflict arrived.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**en.wikipedia.org** — The Wikipedia Arbitration Committee case (2021) exposed how shared governance without boundaries amplifies conflict. Differing views on content moderation led to protracted disputes, not consensus. The community didn't converge on collective intelligence — it exhausted itself through process.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Applica questo pattern quando i confini di governance sono documentati esplicitamente e i domini di responsabilità tra stakeholder sono chiaramente separati. GitLab dimostra che processi trasparenti entro confini definiti riducono l'attrito e permettono decisioni collettive efficaci.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare questo pattern quando i domini decisionali sono indefiniti o quando tutti gli stakeholder si aspettano di avere voce su ogni decisione. Il caso Wikipedia ArbCom dimostra che governance condivisa senza confini amplifica il conflitto invece di risolverlo.
