### Il Dogma

Distributed groups can navigate toward shared goals through organic emergence, allowing multiple pathways to coexist until natural selection reveals the optimal direction. The theory claims peer networks evolve toward shared purpose through collective sensing and response, without centralized planning.

### La Realtà

The Peeragogy Handbook promises emergent roadmaps. Twenty-three patterns, each ending with "What's Next" steps that supposedly aggregate into collective direction. The theory sounds clean — peer networks naturally evolve toward shared goals through distributed sensing and response.

Watch any actual peer project try this. The Linux kernel didn't emerge from collective wisdom — Linus Torvalds made thousands of small dictatorial decisions until subsystem maintainers could carry the load. Wikipedia's early chaos required Jimmy Wales stepping in repeatedly until bureaucratic structures solidified. Not emergence. Governance.

The friction lives in the gap between sensing change and acting on it. Everyone can feel when something's broken. Few will name it publicly. Fewer will volunteer to fix it. The roadmap that "emerges" is usually the path of least resistance — which means the path that avoids the hardest conversations.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if emergence is what you call it when you're too scared to make a decision and hope the universe will do it for you — and the refusal to plan is itself a plan that protects existing power by making change feel impossible?"*

### Evidenze di Faglia

**drupal.org** — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work, not organic emergence. Drupal dimostra che la coordinazione distribuita di successo richiede design istituzionale intenzionale, non attesa paziente.

**w3.org** — Choosing lighter governance models for standards development trades authority for speed, creating different failure modes. WebAssembly mostra che l'emergenza come strategia di governance è un trade-off, non una soluzione.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Applica questo pattern solo quando combinato con strutture di scaffolding esplicite che canalizzano l'emergenza verso decisioni attuabili. Drupal e WebAssembly mostrano che anche i sistemi più distribuiti hanno bisogno di strutture decisionali design.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare questo pattern quando si affrontano pressioni temporali o transizioni di governance. Drupal e WebAssembly dimostrano che l'emergenza organica senza scaffolding produce paralisi, non direzione collettiva.
