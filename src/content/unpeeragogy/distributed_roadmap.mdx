"Pattern that offers another view of the Roadmap project in its emergent form." "We intend to review and extend the patterns."

The Emergent Roadmap promises something beautiful: direction that emerges from the group, not imposed from above.

**Why it matters:** A roadmap that emerges by itself is a roadmap that never emerges. Directions don't emerge. They're chosen. A group waiting for direction to emerge is a group that doesn't move. Because every possibility is valid, none is priority. Debate is open. Consensus is distant. The roadmap stays a draft.

The theory talks about "emergent form." Practice knows "emergent" is an elegant way of saying "we haven't decided." A roadmap doesn't emerge. It's built. It's chosen. It's abandoned. Emergence is a biological metaphor that doesn't apply to human groups. Human groups don't sprout. They decide. Or they don't decide.

**What to do instead:** Emergence works in nature. In groups, it only works if there's a selection mechanism. Someone who says "this option yes, that one no." Without selection, everything emerges and nothing settles. The Emergent Roadmap is an oxymoron. It's either emergent (undecided) or a roadmap (decided). It can't be both.

> **Perturbator:** *"Your roadmap is 'emergent.' That's an elegant way of saying you don't have a clear direction. Not having one is fine. But don't call it a roadmap. Call it a 'list of options.' The difference is a roadmap says 'we're going there.' A list of options says 'we could go there, or there, or stay.' The list of options takes you nowhere."*
