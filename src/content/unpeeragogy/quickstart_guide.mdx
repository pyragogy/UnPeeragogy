### Il Dogma

The Quickstart Guide presents a structured approach for rapid peer learning group formation, emphasizing immediate action over extended planning phases. Groups can quickly establish working relationships and begin productive collaboration without extensive governance setup.

### La Realtà

Fast group formation is like fast food — it fills you up quickly but leaves you hungry an hour later.

The assumption that groups can skip governance setup and go straight to collaboration is the most expensive shortcut in peer learning. GitLab's stewardship handbook shows that explicit governance boundaries reduce friction — but only when documented upfront. Drupal's governance transition took years precisely because initial formation skipped the structural work. The Linux kernel's Reviewed-by system shows that even technical communities need formal quality mechanisms that quickstart guides don't mention.

Quickstart works for a conversation. Not for a project that needs to survive its first disagreement.

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction in open-core projects. GitLab shows that upfront governance documentation is essential even in quick-start contexts.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**drupal.org** — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work. Drupal demonstrates that skipping governance setup produces long-term costs that exceed any short-term gain.

**docs.kernel.org** — Linux kernel's Reviewed-by tag system creates formal peer assessment accountability. Linux shows that even rapid technical projects need formal quality mechanisms that quickstart guides omit.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** La formazione rapida funziona quando i confini di governance sono documentati esplicitamente in anticipo. GitLab mostra che la documentazione chiara dei confini di autorità tra diversi stakeholder riduce l'attrito anche in contesti rapidi.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare questo pattern per progetti collaborativi continuativi o lavoro che richiede validazione di qualità. Drupal mostra che il lavoro comunitario senza strutture di autorità esplicite produce costi di governance a lungo termine che superano qualsiasi guadagno a breve termine.
