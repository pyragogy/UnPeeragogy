### Il Dogma

Sharing is a first step but does not lead to creation. Groups that only share resources do not produce anything new, creating a library-like environment instead of a laboratory.

### La Realtà

A teachers' group shares worksheets religiously. Google Drive full of lesson plans. Shared folders organized by grade, subject, standard. Two hundred teachers contributing. Zero new curricula produced.

The pattern correctly identifies the failure: sharing without structure is curation, not creation. But it misses the enabling conditions. P2PU's learning circles show that sharing with defined facilitator roles and institutional partnerships produces sustainable learning — not because sharing is replaced, but because sharing is structured. Drupal's governance transition required intentional structural work to move from sharing resources to creating them together. WebAssembly shows that lightweight governance creates the sharing-without-creation trap.

The problem isn't sharing. It's sharing without a plan for what happens next.

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**p2pu.org** — Peer learning models with defined facilitator roles and institutional partnerships are more sustainable than purely peer-driven sharing. P2PU demonstrates that structured sharing produces creation.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**drupal.org** — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work. Drupal shows that moving from sharing to creation requires intentional governance work.

**w3.org** — Choosing lighter governance models for standards development trades authority for speed. WebAssembly demonstrates that lightweight governance creates the sharing-without-creation trap.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Il pattern prevede accuratamente il fallimento quando la condivisione manca di struttura di governance o definizione di ruoli. Drupal mostra che il lavoro strutturale intenzionale, non l'emergenza organica, è necessario per passare dalla condivisione alla creazione.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare questo pattern quando la condivisione include ruoli di facilitazione definiti o partnership istituzionali. P2PU mostra che il peer learning strutturato con ruoli di facilitazione e partnership produce creazione sostenibile.
