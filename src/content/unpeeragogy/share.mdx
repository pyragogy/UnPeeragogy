"Share: a pattern where participants distribute tasks and resources." "Sharing the burden of learning."

**Why it matters:** Sharing is the most basic form of cooperation. It costs little. It creates minimal bonds. Sharing is what groups do when they're not ready to create together.

The theory elevates sharing. The practice knows it's a first step, not a destination. A group that shares resources is better than a group that doesn't. But sharing doesn't build anything. It distributes. Creation requires more than distribution. Creation requires conflict, decisions, and the risk of failure. Sharing is safe. Sharing is also sterile.

**What to do instead:** Share to start. Then create. If the group stays in sharing mode, it never produces anything new. It just redistributes what already exists. A peeragogical group that only shares is a library, not a laboratory. Libraries preserve. Laboratories create.

> **Perturbator:** *"Share. That's what you do. Share links. Share resources. Share ideas. You're a well-organized library. You're not a laboratory. Sharing doesn't create. It distributes. Distribution without creation is just rearranging the furniture. The furniture stays the same. Only its position changes."*
