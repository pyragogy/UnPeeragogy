### Il Dogma

The Peeragogy Workbook presents itself as an open invitation to collaborative learning, designed to welcome newcomers into peer-to-peer educational practices through accessible introductory content.

### La Realtà

Nothing says "peer learning" like a 20-page manual on how to be peers.

The workbook's open invitation assumes that accessibility of content creates accessibility of participation. It doesn't. GitLab's stewardship handbook shows that documented boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduce friction more effectively than extensive welcome content. Apache Software Foundation's meritocracy governance shows that even open systems create de facto hierarchies through time availability and domain expertise.

The welcome is necessary. It's also insufficient. The workbook that focuses on welcoming without addressing the structural barriers to participation is like a greeter at a building with no ramp.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if nothing says 'peer learning' like a 20-page manual on how to be peers — and a welcome without structural access is like a greeter at a building with no ramp?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries reduces friction. GitLab shows that documented participation boundaries welcome newcomers more effectively than extensive introductory content.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**microbit.org** — BBC micro:bit foundation governance shows that even intentional educational foundations face participation barriers that welcome content cannot address.

**apache.org** — Apache Software Foundation governance demonstrates that meritocracy-based systems create de facto hierarchies through time availability — the most welcome content cannot compensate for structural barriers.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** L'accoglienza funziona quando i confini di governance sono resi espliciti e le strutture di partecipazione tengono conto della diversa disponibilità di tempo. GitLab mostra che confini documentati tra diversi stakeholder riducono l'attrito più dei contenuti di benvenuto.

**Regola Negativa:** Non assumere che la governance peer sia naturalmente egualitaria o che contenuti di benvenuto estesi migliorino l'accessibilità. Apache mostra che i sistemi basati su merito creano gerarchie di fatto.
