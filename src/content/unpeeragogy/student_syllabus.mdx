### The Assumption

Learners can self-organize around shared learning objectives when given basic structural guidance, creating emergent peer-to-peer educational experiences that adapt organically to participant needs.

### Operational Reality

Calling it a "student syllabus" does not make the students equal. Someone still has to decide what gets learned and when.

The vault records show the pattern failing through knowledge-hoarding when feedback loops are absent, decision-stagnation when structures become fossilized, and authority-attrition when traditional roles dissolve without replacement. GitLab's stewardship handbook shows that clear documentation of decision-making roles prevents all three failure modes. Drupal's governance transition demonstrates that undefined authority produces the exact stagnation the vault records.

The student syllabus works when the structure is designed for evolution, not for permanence. The problem is not the syllabus — it is treating the syllabus as a finished object rather than a living agreement.

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if calling it a 'student syllabus' doesn't make the students equal — and someone still has to decide what gets learned and when?"*

### Empirical Evidence

**Resilience Cases (Confirming):**
- [GitLab Stewardship Handbook](https://about.gitlab.com/handbook/company/stewardship/) — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction. Clear documentation of decision-making roles prevents knowledge-hoarding and decision-stagnation.

**Fracture Cases (Complicating):**
- [Drupal Governance](https://www.drupal.org/governance) — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work. Undefined authority without clear evolutionary design produces stagnation.

### Operational Heuristics

**Positive Boundary:** The student syllabus works when governance boundaries are documented explicitly and structural transitions are designed intentionally — clear documentation of decision-making roles prevents knowledge-hoarding.

**Negative Boundary:** Do not apply this pattern when governance boundaries are unclear or feedback mechanisms are absent — undefined authority leads to knowledge-hoarding and decision-stagnation.

### Epistemic Synthesis

The student syllabus works when the structure is designed for evolution, not for permanence. The problem is not the syllabus — it is treating the syllabus as a finished object rather than a living agreement. GitLab prevents failure modes through clear authority documentation. Drupal produces them when authority is undefined. Calling it a "student syllabus" does not make the students equal.
