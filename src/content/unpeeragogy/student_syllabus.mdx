### Il Dogma

Learners can self-organize around shared learning objectives when given basic structural guidance, creating emergent peer-to-peer educational experiences that adapt organically to participant needs.

### La Realtà

Calling it a "student syllabus" doesn't make the students equal. Someone still has to decide what gets learned and when.

The vault records show the pattern failing through knowledge-hoarding when feedback loops are absent, decision-stagnation when structures become fossilized, and authority-attrition when traditional roles dissolve without replacement. GitLab's stewardship handbook shows that clear documentation of decision-making roles prevents all three failure modes. Drupal's governance transition demonstrates that undefined authority produces the exact stagnation the vault records.

The student syllabus works when the structure is designed for evolution, not for permanence. The problem isn't the syllabus — it's treating the syllabus as a finished object rather than a living agreement.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Il syllabus studente funziona quando i confini di governance sono documentati esplicitamente e le transizioni strutturali sono progettate intenzionalmente. GitLab mostra che la documentazione chiara dei ruoli decisionali previene il knowledge-hoarding.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare questo pattern quando i confini di governance non sono chiari o i meccanismi di feedback sono assenti. I vault incidents mostrano che l'autorità indefinita porta a knowledge-hoarding e decision-stagnation.
