### Il Dogma

Curated reading lists bootstrap peer learning communities by creating shared intellectual resources and common ground for collaborative knowledge building.

### La Realtà

Nothing kills peer learning faster than a syllabus. The moment you assign homework, you've created teachers and students.

The handbook incidents show that recommended reading creates guilt, decision paralysis, and preference for action over preparation. Not because reading is bad. Because reading lists establish a hierarchy of knowledge that contradicts the egalitarian premise of peer learning. Someone chose these texts. Someone decided they matter more than other texts. Someone defined what "informed participation" looks like.

GitLab's stewardship handbook works because it documents operational boundaries — not required reading. Wikipedia's Good Article process works through reviewable work, not reading prerequisites. Drupal's governance shows that when reading becomes an entry requirement, it selects for people with time to read rather than people with something to contribute.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if nothing kills peer learning faster than a syllabus — and the moment you assign homework, you've created teachers and students?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction in open-core projects. GitLab shows that operational documentation outperforms curated reading for community building.

**en.wikipedia.org** — Peer assessment with transparent criteria and independent reviewers creates sustainable quality tiers. Wikipedia demonstrates that peer learning based on reviewable work outperforms reading-based communities.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**drupal.org** — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work. Drupal shows that reading-based entry requirements select for time availability, not contribution potential.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Le liste di lettura funzionano quando la lettura emerge dalla pratica e mantiene status opzionale. Wikipedia mostra che il peer assessment con criteri trasparenti crea qualità sostenibile senza richiedere prerequisite di lettura.

**Regola Negativa:** Non usare la lettura raccomandata come requisito di ingresso alla comunità. I handbook incidents mostrano che i prerequisiti di lettura creano gerarchie di status e paralisi decisionale, trasformando le relazioni peer in relazioni insegnante-studente.

**Stato:** PROVVISORIO

Nulla uccide il peer learning più velocemente di un syllabus. La lettura raccomandata stabilisce una gerarchia di conoscenza che contraddice la premessa egualitaria del peer learning. Qualcuno ha scelto questi testi. Qualcuno ha deciso che contano più di altri. GitLab funziona perché documenta confini operativi — non letture obbligatorie. Wikipedia funziona attraverso lavoro revisionabile, non prerequisiti di lettura.
