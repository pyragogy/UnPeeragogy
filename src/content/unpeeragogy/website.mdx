### Il Dogma

Websites are static documents that fail to demonstrate peeragogy principles, functioning as repositories rather than communities.

### La Realtà

Static websites don't demonstrate peeragogy. Neither do websites that pretend hierarchy doesn't exist.

P2PU's learning circles show that peer learning sustainability comes from defined facilitator roles and institutional partnerships — not from website features. GitLab's stewardship handbook documents governance boundaries that make the website a boundary object, not a community container. P2P University's governance challenges and the cMOOC divide show that peer governance without institutional scaffolding hits credentialing crises regardless of website design.

The pattern assumes the website is the problem. It's not. A static site with clear governance works better than a dynamic site with confused authority. The medium doesn't determine the peer learning — the governance does.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Il sito web funziona quando i confini di governance sono documentati esplicitamente e le partnership istituzionali forniscono scaffolding per la partecipazione peer. P2PU e GitLab mostrano che la governance, non la piattaforma, determina l'efficacia.

**Regola Negativa:** Non assumere che la governance peer pura possa scalare senza scaffolding istituzionale. Il divide cMOOC vs xMOOC mostra che la governance peer senza meccanismi di validazione formali colpisce ceiling strutturali.
