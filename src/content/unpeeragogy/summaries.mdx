### Il Dogma

Summaries indicate the original content is too verbose, that summaries written by the same authors lead to biased essentiality, and that good summaries require an outsider's perspective.

### La Realtà

Every summary is a coup attempt in disguise.

The pattern correctly identifies that summarization is not neutral — the summarizer decides what matters. GitLab's stewardship handbook works because the boundaries it summarizes are explicit and contestable. LWN.net's kernel summaries are trusted because they're produced by independent third parties with formal editorial processes. But Wikipedia Signpost and Debian DPL newsletters show that community-produced summaries become de facto governance channels — the summarizer's attention becomes the project's priorities.

Summarization is curation, and curation is power. The pattern identifies the bias. It doesn't acknowledge that the solution isn't better summarizers — it's governance that makes summary effects visible and contestable.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if every summary is a coup attempt in disguise — and summarization is curation, and curation is power?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries reduces friction. GitLab shows that formal constraints on summary authority reduce bias effects.

**lwn.net** — Independent third-party summaries create shared awareness. LWN demonstrates that formal editorial processes produce trusted summaries.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**en.wikipedia.org** — Wikipedia Signpost community journalism becomes de facto governance channel — the summarizer's attention shapes project priorities.

**lists.debian.org** — Debian DPL bits newsletter shows that centralized summary authorship creates hidden editorial control where what gets summarized defines what gets done.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Le summary funzionano quando l'indipendenza del summarizer è istituzionalizzata o l'autorità è esplicitamente delimitata. GitLab e LWN mostrano che vincoli formali sul potere di summary e processi editoriali indipendenti producono sintesi affidabili.

**Regola Negativa:** Non creare summary senza confini di governance espliciti. Wikipedia Signpost e Debian DPL mostrano che le summary prodotte dalla comunità diventano canali di governance de facto.
