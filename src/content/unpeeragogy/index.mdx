## Summary

Pattern catalogs promise equal access to all techniques. Theory says documentation enables selection. Practice shows undifferentiated indexes kill projects — you pick the wrong tool because nothing warns you it's broken.

## Euristiche Operative

### Positive Rule

> This index works when it explicitly documents governance boundaries and structural requirements for each pattern. The GitLab stewardship handbook shows that explicit documentation of pattern constraints and success conditions enables effective pattern selection and implementation.

### Negative Rule

> Do not present all patterns as equally viable when evidence shows distinct failure modes. The MakerDAO Black Thursday crisis and Drupal's governance transition show that pattern selection without validation context leads to catastrophic misapplication in high-stakes situations.

## Sintesi Obliqua

The index claims all patterns work if you follow the steps. Partial verification — when governance boundaries get explicit documentation, like GitLab's stewardship handbook, friction drops and communities function. But Drupal needed years of structural work to transition from BDFL to community governance, and MakerDAO's governance mechanisms collapsed under Black Thursday stress. The missing piece: failure mode documentation. Patterns without headstones.
