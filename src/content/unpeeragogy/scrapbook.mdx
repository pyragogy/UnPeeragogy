### Il Dogma

The Scrapbook pattern describes a temporary holding space for items that don't yet fit into the project's taxonomy. Items accumulate here until the structure is revised to accommodate them, serving as a signal that organizational categories need updating.

### La Realtà

The scrapbook isn't temporary storage. It's where good intentions go to die.

The vault records show scrapbooks failing through decision-stagnation and authority-attrition. Items accumulate. Categories are never revised. The scrapbook becomes a permanent holding pattern — a monument to the group's inability to decide what matters. GitLab's stewardship handbook shows that explicit governance boundaries enable smooth organizational evolution. Drupal's governance transition demonstrates that without authority to revise structure, scrapbook items accumulate until they define the project by weight of what couldn't be categorized.

The scrapbook pattern assumes organizational categories will evolve naturally. They don't. They require someone with authority to decide when the structure has changed enough.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if the scrapbook isn't temporary storage — it's where good intentions go to die — and items accumulate because nobody has the authority to decide what they actually are?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction. GitLab shows that clear authority boundaries enable smooth organizational evolution without permanent holding patterns.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**drupal.org** — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work. Drupal demonstrates that without authority to revise structure, scrapbook items accumulate until they define the project by weight.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Lo scrapbook funziona quando confini di governance espliciti definiscono chi ha l'autorità di rivedere la struttura organizzativa. GitLab mostra che confini di autorità chiari permettono evoluzione organizzativa fluida.

**Regola Negativa:** Non implementare scrapbook quando l'autorità tassonomica non è chiara o è contestata. Il pattern assume evoluzione organizzativa naturale, ma l'evidenza mostra che senza confini di governance espliciti, lo scrapbook diventa un buco nero permanente.
