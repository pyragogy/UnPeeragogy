"Scrapbook: items that don't fit in the main sections." A miscellaneous page.

**Why it matters:** A scrapbook is a waste bin. Items that didn't fit anywhere else. It's the page that proves the Handbook doesn't have a clear taxonomy. If you need a scrapbook, your structure is incomplete.

The problem with scrapbooks is that they grow. Items accumulate. Nothing is removed. The scrapbook becomes a second main section. And it's harder to navigate than the main one because it has no structure. It's a pile.

**What to do instead:** A scrapbook is a sign that you need to revise the structure. Every item in the scrapbook should be assigned to an existing section. If it doesn't fit, the taxonomy is wrong. Not the item. Fix the taxonomy. The scrapbook is a symptom. The cure is structural revision.

> **Perturbator:** *"Your scrapbook has 15 items. Each one is a thing that didn't fit. That doesn't mean the items are strange. It means your categories are incomplete. A scrapbook is a confession: 'we don't know how to organize what we have.' The scrapbook grows until the structure is revised. But revision is work. So the scrapbook grows."*
