### The Assumption

The Scrapbook pattern describes a temporary holding space for items that do not yet fit into the project's taxonomy. Items accumulate here until the structure is revised to accommodate them, serving as a signal that organizational categories need updating.

### Operational Reality

The scrapbook is not temporary storage. It is where good intentions go to die.

The vault records show scrapbooks failing through decision-stagnation and authority-attrition. Items accumulate. Categories are never revised. The scrapbook becomes a permanent holding pattern — a monument to the group's inability to decide what matters. GitLab's stewardship handbook shows that explicit governance boundaries enable smooth organizational evolution. Drupal's governance transition demonstrates that without authority to revise structure, scrapbook items accumulate until they define the project by weight of what could not be categorized.

The scrapbook pattern assumes organizational categories will evolve naturally. They do not. They require someone with authority to decide when the structure has changed enough.

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if the scrapbook isn't temporary storage — it's where good intentions go to die — and items accumulate because nobody has the authority to decide what they actually are?"*

### Empirical Evidence

**Resilience Cases (Confirming):**
- [GitLab Stewardship Handbook](https://about.gitlab.com/handbook/company/stewardship/) — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction. Clear authority boundaries enable smooth organizational evolution without permanent holding patterns.

**Fracture Cases (Complicating):**
- [Drupal Governance](https://www.drupal.org/governance) — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work. Without authority to revise structure, scrapbook items accumulate until they define the project by weight.

### Operational Heuristics

**Positive Boundary:** The scrapbook works when explicit governance boundaries define who has authority to revise organizational structure — clear authority boundaries enable smooth organizational evolution.

**Negative Boundary:** Do not implement a scrapbook when taxonomic authority is unclear or contested — the pattern assumes natural organizational evolution, but evidence shows that without explicit governance boundaries, the scrapbook becomes a permanent black hole.

### Epistemic Synthesis

The scrapbook is not temporary storage. It is where good intentions go to die. GitLab shows that clear authority boundaries enable smooth organizational evolution. Drupal shows that without authority to revise structure, scrapbook items accumulate until they define the project by weight of what could not be categorized. Categories do not evolve naturally. They require someone with authority to decide when the structure has changed enough.
