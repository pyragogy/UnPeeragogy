### Il Dogma

Workscapes provide informal learning opportunities where knowledge sharing and skill development occur through daily work interactions, collaborative problem-solving, and ambient learning within organizations.

### La Realtà

Informal learning is what management calls it when they don't want to pay for training.

P2PU's learning circles survive through institutional partnerships that provide structure — demonstrated informal learning. GitLab's stewardship handbook shows that explicit governance boundaries make informal learning effective by defining who can learn from whom. The BBC micro:bit foundation and the cMOOC divide show that informal learning without institutional scaffolding produces credentialing crises — the learning happens but nobody can prove it.

The pattern assumes workscape learning is organic. The evidence shows it requires as much structure as formal learning — just different structure. The difference is that workscape structure is invisible, which makes it harder to contest and easier to get wrong.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if informal learning is what management calls it when they don't want to pay for training — and workscape learning requires as much structure as formal learning, just different structure?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**p2pu.org** — Peer learning with defined facilitator roles and institutional partnerships is more sustainable. P2PU shows that informal learning requires institutional scaffolding to survive.

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries reduces friction. GitLab shows that governance boundaries make informal learning effective by defining who can learn from whom.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**microbit.org** — BBC micro:bit foundation governance shows informal learning without credentialing produces recognition gaps.

**researchgate.net** — cMOOC vs xMOOC divide demonstrates that informal learning validation without institutional scaffolding produces crisis when learners need to demonstrate competence.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Il workscape funziona quando abbinato a partnership istituzionali che forniscono validazione e struttura. P2PU e GitLab mostrano che lo scaffolding istituzionale e i confini di governance espliciti permettono l'apprendimento informale.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare questo pattern per creare apprendimento senza riconoscimento formale. BBC micro:bit e cMOOC mostrano che l'apprendimento informale senza scaffolding produce crisi di credenziali.
