### The Assumption

Workscapes provide informal learning opportunities where knowledge sharing and skill development occur through daily work interactions, collaborative problem-solving, and ambient learning within organizations.

### Operational Reality

Informal learning is what management calls it when they do not want to pay for training.

P2PU's learning circles survive through institutional partnerships that provide structure — demonstrating that informal learning requires scaffolding. GitLab's stewardship handbook shows that explicit governance boundaries make informal learning effective by defining who can learn from whom. P2P University's governance challenges show that informal learning without institutional scaffolding produces credentialing crises — the learning happens but nobody can prove it. WebAssembly shows that informal governance structures hit ceilings that require formalization.

The pattern assumes workscape learning is organic. The evidence shows it requires as much structure as formal learning — just different structure. The difference is that workscape structure is invisible, which makes it harder to contest and easier to get wrong.

### Empirical Evidence

**Resilience Cases (Confirming):**
- [P2PU Learning Circles](https://www.p2pu.org/en/learning-circles/) — Peer learning with defined facilitator roles and institutional partnerships is more sustainable than purely peer-driven learning. Informal learning requires institutional scaffolding to survive.
- [GitLab Stewardship Handbook](https://about.gitlab.com/handbook/company/stewardship/) — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries reduces friction. Governance boundaries make informal learning effective by defining who can learn from whom.

**Fracture Cases (Complicating):**
- [P2PU About / Governance Challenges](https://www.p2pu.org/en/about/) — Informal learning without institutional scaffolding produces credentialing crises — the learning happens but nobody can prove it.
- [WebAssembly Community Group](https://www.w3.org/community/webassembly/) — Informal governance structures hit ceilings that require formalization.

### Operational Heuristics

**Positive Boundary:** Workscape works when paired with institutional partnerships that provide validation and structure — institutional scaffolding and explicit governance boundaries enable informal learning.

**Negative Boundary:** Do not apply this pattern to create learning without formal recognition — informal learning without scaffolding produces credentialing crises when learners need to demonstrate competence.

### Epistemic Synthesis

The pattern assumes workscape learning is organic. The evidence shows it requires as much structure as formal learning — just different structure. P2PU shows that informal learning requires institutional scaffolding. GitLab shows that governance boundaries define who can learn from whom. The difference is that workscape structure is invisible, which makes it harder to contest and easier to get wrong.
