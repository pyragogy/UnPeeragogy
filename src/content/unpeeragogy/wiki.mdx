### Il Dogma

Wikis are the natural tools for peeragogical collaboration due to their openness, editability, and accessible structure, enabling communities to co-create knowledge with minimal barriers to participation.

### La Realtà

Wikis don't create collaboration. They create graveyards of unfinished, conflicted, or abandoned knowledge. The editing interface is simple enough to invite contribution and complex enough to produce contradiction.

OpenStreetMap's Data Working Group resolves wiki disputes through binding authority — the wiki is a content layer, not a governance layer. GitLab's stewardship handbook shows that explicit governance boundaries reduce friction more effectively than wiki features. Debian's init system transition shows that wiki edits without recourse produce contested outcomes that feel imposed.

The wiki is a technology. Collaboration is a governance outcome. The pattern mistakes the technology for the outcome.

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**wiki.osmfoundation.org** — Formal dispute resolution bodies resolve wiki disputes. OpenStreetMap shows that binding authority makes wiki collaboration productive by providing escalation paths.

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries reduces friction. GitLab demonstrates that governance determines wiki effectiveness, not the reverse.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**wiki.debian.org** — Debian's init system transition shows that wiki edits without recourse produce contested outcomes. Debian shows that wiki modifications without escalation produce conflict.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Il wiki funziona quando è abbinato a meccanismi formali di risoluzione dispute e confini di governance espliciti. OpenStreetMap e GitLab mostrano che l'autorità vincolante per la risoluzione dispute rende il wiki produttivo.

**Regola Negativa:** Non implementare wiki come meccanismo di governance. Debian mostra che la modifica wiki senza escalation produce conflitto. Il wiki è un layer di contenuti, non un layer di governance.
