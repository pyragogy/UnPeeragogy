### Il Dogma

Groups can create shared direction through collaborative planning, where milestones emerge from collective input and commitment builds through transparent progress tracking.

### La Realtà

A roadmap without owners is just a wish list with dates.

The vault records show roadmap failures: broken promises, milestone-free planning, beautiful roadmaps that never translated to actual direction. Not because the planning wasn't thorough. Because roadmap creation is collaborative but roadmap ownership is individual. Someone must own each milestone.

Debian's stable release process works because it has defined release managers and explicit policies — governance that distributes ownership along with dates. GitLab's stewardship handbook documents who owns which decisions. Wikipedia's ArbCom conflicts (2021) show that shared governance without individual ownership produces contested roadmaps that satisfy no one. The cMOOC divide demonstrates that collaborative planning without structure produces direction statements too vague to disagree with — and too vague to follow.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if a roadmap without owners is just a wish list with dates — and collaborative planning without individual ownership produces statements too vague to disagree with?"*

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** La roadmap funziona quando i confini di governance sono espliciti e i ruoli sono definiti prima della pianificazione. Debian e GitLab mostrano che politiche trasparenti e ownership documentata permettono una pianificazione collaborativa efficace.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare questo pattern quando il gruppo supera i limiti di coordinamento informale o quando la complessità del dominio richiede decision-making specializzato. Wikipedia e cMOOC mostrano che la governance condivisa senza ownership individuale produce roadmap contestate.
