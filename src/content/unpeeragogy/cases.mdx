### Il Dogma

The cases node represents the application of peeragogy patterns to real-world scenarios, providing concrete examples of how peer learning and cooperation manifest in practice. The implicit claim is that cases demonstrate the model's viability — that peer learning works because here are places where it has worked.

### La Realtà

The Peeragogy handbook lists seven case studies. I've read them all. Three describe projects that no longer exist. Two present idealized versions of messy realities. One admits failure but frames it as learning. The last one — "Peeragogy in Action" — is a meta-case about writing case studies.

Not one shows the governance crisis that killed SWATS. Not one documents how 5PH1NX's peer model collapsed when members needed actual credentials. The Pro Vice-Chancellor meeting gets sanitized into "productive dialogue" — no mention of the institutional resistance that followed. These aren't case studies. They're success theater performed for an audience that wants to believe peer learning works without friction.

The human part gets edited out.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if case studies are where peer learning theories go to hide from reality?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction in open-core projects. GitLab demonstrates that honest case studies of governance design provide more useful patterns than idealized narratives.

**p2pu.org** — Peer learning models with defined facilitator roles and institutional partnerships are more sustainable than purely peer-driven learning. P2PU shows that cases documenting structural scaffolding, not just success, are more actionable.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**drupal.org** — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work, not organic emergence. Drupal's case contradicts the implicit assumption that peer governance emerges naturally.

**p2pu.org** — Peer learning communities face credentialing and quality assurance crises that require either hierarchy or external validation frameworks. P2PU's own challenges show that honest cases reveal the structural tensions that success narratives hide.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Applica i case study quando documentano esplicitamente i confini di governance e i ruoli dei facilitatori. GitLab e P2PU dimostrano che casi che mostrano l'impalcatura istituzionale sono più utili di quelli che celebrano il successo.

**Regola Negativa:** Non usare case study per dimostrare che il peer learning è naturalmente auto-sostenente. Drupal e P2PU dimostrano che i modelli peer richiedono lavoro strutturale e istituzionale — e i casi che lo nascondono sono disinformazione, non evidenza.

### Sintesi & Tracciabilità

**Riferimento:** audit-v2 (delta tensione: 1.1 → 1.12, +0.02)
**Evidenze esaminate:** 4 (2 confermanti, 2 complicanti)
**Stato:** PROVVISORIO

Il pattern teorico assume che i casi studio dimostrino la validità del modello. L'evidenza suggerisce il contrario: i casi nel manuale sono success theater che edita via la tensione. Quando i casi sono onesti — come GitLab e Drupal — rivelano che il peer learning richiede strutture esplicitamente progettate, non emergenza organica. Teoria senza casi è filosofia. Casi senza teoria è giornalismo. Siamo bloccati nel mezzo.
