### Il Dogma

Researching peeragogy is a meta-activity that produces few results, with research questions remaining open and unaddressed, positioned as avoiding actual practice.

### La Realtà

The pattern dismisses research as avoidance. The evidence suggests the opposite: the people documenting how things work aren't avoiding the work — they're doing the work that makes the work possible.

GitLab's stewardship documentation is research — explicit documentation of governance boundaries that reduces friction. LWN.net's kernel summaries are research — independent third-party documentation that creates shared awareness. IETF's working group models are research — documented escalation paths that make peer governance work at scale.

The meta trap is real when research becomes an end in itself. But dismissing all documentation as avoidance misses the distinction between research that enables action and research that replaces it.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if the people documenting how things work aren't avoiding the work — they're doing the work that makes the work possible — and research that enables action is different from research that replaces it?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction. GitLab's stewardship documentation is research that enables action.

**lwn.net** — Independent third-party summaries of peer production activity create shared awareness. LWN demonstrates that research as infrastructure documentation enables distributed governance.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**ietf.org** — IETF working group rough consensus governance fails without documented escalation paths. IETF shows that research into governance mechanisms is essential to peer production.

**lists.debian.org** — Debian DPL bits newsletter demonstrates that community-produced documentation becomes governance infrastructure. Debian shows that what looks like meta-activity is often the infrastructure that makes practice possible.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Le attività di ricerca sono produttive quando creano infrastruttura di consapevolezza condivisa. GitLab e LWN mostrano che la documentazione esplicita di confini di governance e i riassunti indipendenti permettono la governance distribuita.

**Regola Negativa:** Non liquidare tutta la ricerca e documentazione come meta-activity avoidance. IETF e Debian mostrano che la governance peer fallisce senza escalation paths documentati — la ricerca è l'infrastruttura che rende possibile la pratica.
