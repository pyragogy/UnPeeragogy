"Heartbeat: a regular meeting or event that keeps the group alive." "The group's pulse."

A regular meeting. A predictable rhythm. The heartbeat pattern is the simplest and most necessary.

**Why it matters:** The heartbeat is the group's calendar presence. If it stops, the group decays. Without a fixed appointment, there's no urgency. No reason to show up. The heartbeat creates a container for all other patterns.

The problem with heartbeats is that they become routine. Routine sustains but doesn't advance. A group with a strong heartbeat can stay alive for years without producing anything. It gathers every week. It talks. It disconnects. Nothing changes. The heartbeat becomes the purpose. "We meet" replaces "we produce."

**What to do instead:** The heartbeat is necessary but not sufficient. It keeps the group alive. It doesn't make it grow. Growth requires rupture. A heartbeat that's too regular suppresses emergence. Schedule disruption. Cancel a meeting to make a decision. Change the rhythm. A predictable heartbeat is a comfortable group. A comfortable group doesn't change.

> **Perturbator:** *"Your group meets every Tuesday at 6. For two years. Same people. Same agenda. Same nothing. The heartbeat is strong. The group is alive. The group also isn't growing. A heartbeat keeps things alive. It doesn't make things grow. Growth requires disruption. A heartbeat that's too regular is a lullaby. It puts the group to sleep."*
