### Il Dogma

Collective curation through peer tagging and sharing creates emergent quality control and knowledge organization.

### La Realtà

Crowdsourcing without governance is just a fancy way to create a mess that nobody wants to clean up.

The pattern assumes that peer tagging will naturally produce useful organization. Mondragon's worker cooperative governance — 70+ years of large-scale coordination — uses formal councils and representative democracy, not peer consensus. GitLab's stewardship handbook documents explicit authority boundaries without which tagging produces noise, not signal. Wikipedia's ArbCom exists because peer curation without governance creates disputes that tagging cannot resolve.

Social bookmarking works at small scale with aligned interests. At any scale involving divergent interests or contested categories, it produces the mess the pattern describes — but the pattern mistakes this for a technology problem when it's a governance problem.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if crowdsourcing without governance is just a fancy way to create a mess that nobody wants to clean up — and the problem isn't the tags, it's who decides what they mean?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction. GitLab shows that governance scaffolding precedes effective peer curation.

**mondragon-corporation.com** — Mondragon cooperative governance demonstrates that even cooperative organizations require representative democracy and formal councils, not peer consensus.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**mondragon-corporation.com** — Mondragon worker cooperative governance shows that successful large-scale coordination uses formal councils and representative democracy — not emergent peer curation.

**en.wikipedia.org** — Wikipedia's Arbitration Committee demonstrates that peer curation without governance creates disputes that tagging cannot resolve.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** La curation collettiva funziona quando confini di governance espliciti e processi decisionali sono stabiliti prima che la partecipazione peer inizi. GitLab mostra che l'autorità documentata e i confini tra stakeholder riducono l'attrito nella curation.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare questo pattern a scale maggiori senza strutture di governance formali. Mondragon mostra che anche le organizzazioni cooperative richiedono democrazia rappresentativa e consigli formali — l'emergenza peer non scala.
