The foreword sets the stage. It tells you why you should care.

**Why it matters:** A foreword is an endorsement. Someone credible says "this is worth your time." It's a social signal, not content.

The Handbook doesn't have a formal foreword from an external authority. Its "foreword" is more of an introduction. That's honest. It means the project couldn't (or didn't want to) get someone famous to endorse it. The work stands on its own.

**What to do instead:** A real foreword would help the Handbook reach people outside its circle. If someone with credibility said "this worked for my group," it would mean more than the 300 pages of theory. External validation isn't everything. But it's not nothing. The Handbook stands alone. Admirable. Also lonely.

> **Perturbator:** *"No foreword. No external endorsement. The Handbook stands alone. Admirable independence. Also practical isolation. A foreword from someone credible would have opened doors. The Handbook chose not to ask. Or asked and got no answer. Either way, the absence is a statement: 'our work must be judged on its own.' A brave statement. Also a lonely one."*
