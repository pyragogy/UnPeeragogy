### Il Dogma

Play is often undervalued in peeragogical groups — groups which don't play together tend to dissolve during crises, with play serving as a trust-building mechanism that prevents knowledge hoarding.

### La Realtà

Friday afternoon drinks. The team-building offsite. The icebreaker that's supposed to make everyone trust each other. These rituals create comfort, not resilience. The trust built through games evaporates the moment the budget is cut and someone has to decide whose feature ships.

Not because the games were bad. Not because the team doesn't genuinely like each other. Because play builds social bonds, and social bonds withstand everything except resource conflict.

GitLab's stewardship handbook documents what happens when play meets reality: explicit governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community. OpenStreetMap's Data Working Group resolves disputes through binding authority, not social trust. The Node.js/io.js merger and Drupal's BDFL transition show that no amount of informal bonding survives structural conflict without governance scaffolding.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if trust built through games doesn't survive arguments about money or control — and play is the warm-up, not the workout?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries between corporate sponsor and community reduces friction in open-core projects. GitLab shows that formal governance scaffolding enables play without making it bear the weight of conflict resolution.

**wiki.osmfoundation.org** — Formal dispute resolution bodies with binding authority and published reasoning resolve peer production conflicts that informal consensus cannot. OpenStreetMap demonstrates that social bonds alone cannot resolve structural tension.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**drupal.org** — Intentional governance transition from BDFL to community model required years of structural work, not organic emergence. Drupal shows that play-based trust doesn't survive structural transitions without governance scaffolding.

**sdtimes.com** — Node.js/io.js merger required formal governance restructuring. Node.js demonstrates that even well-bonded communities fragment when resource allocation decisions cannot be made through consensus.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Il gioco funziona quando combinato con strutture di governance esplicite che si attivano durante i conflitti. GitLab e OpenStreetMap mostrano che la risoluzione dispute formale permette al gioco di essere gioco — senza dover portare il peso delle decisioni difficili.

**Regola Negativa:** Non fare affidamento solo sul play-based trust building quando i gruppi affrontano conflitti di risorse o crisi di governance. Node.js/io.js e Drupal mostrano che i legami sociali informali non possono sostituire strutture decisionali formali.
