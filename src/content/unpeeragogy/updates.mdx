### Il Dogma

Regular updates in peer learning projects serve as health indicators and engagement mechanisms, with update frequency correlating to project vitality and community participation.

### La Realtà

Your weekly standup isn't transparency. It's editorial control disguised as process.

GitLab's stewardship handbook and KDE's Community Council show that transparent governance structures make updates trustworthy by distributing agenda-setting power. But Debian's DPL newsletter reveals the hidden cost: centralized update authorship creates editorial control where what gets summarized defines what gets done. WebAssembly's Community Group shows that updates without governance become performance — a ritual of transparency that obscures more than it reveals.

The pattern assumes update frequency correlates with project health. The evidence shows that update governance — who decides what gets reported — correlates with project health far more strongly.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Gli update funzionano come indicatori di salute quando i confini di governance sono espliciti e i processi di update sono strutturati. GitLab e KDE mostrano che la governance trasparente e la distribuzione del potere di agenda-setting rendono gli update affidabili.

**Regola Negativa:** Non implementare update regolari senza distribuire il potere di agenda-setting. Debian mostra che la paternità centralizzata degli update crea controllo editoriale nascosto.
