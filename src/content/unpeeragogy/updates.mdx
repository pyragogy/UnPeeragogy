"Updates on the Peeragogy project: what's new, what's changed." A blog-like section.

**Why it matters:** Updates are the pulse of the project. They say what's happening. They also reveal what's not happening. If the last update is from 2022, the project hasn't been active.

The Handbook's updates section is irregular. Some years have multiple updates. Others have none. The update frequency is a proxy for project health. Healthy projects update regularly. Projects that are stalling update less. The updates tell the story of the project's energy without meaning to.

**What to do instead:** Updates should be regular. Even if there's nothing new. "Nothing new" is an update. It says "we're still here but not producing." Silence says nothing. Silence is worse. A group that doesn't update is a group that doesn't want to be seen. And not wanting to be seen is a sign of trouble.

> **Perturbator:** *"The last update was six months ago. The project isn't dead. It's sleeping. Maybe. Or maybe it's dead and no one has announced it. The updates tell the truth about the project's energy. Check the update frequency. It's the most honest metric. Updates are the heartbeat. If they're silent, the heart has stopped."*
