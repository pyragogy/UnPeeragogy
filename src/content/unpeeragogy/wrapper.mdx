### Il Dogma

The wrapper's casual framing positions peeragogical concepts as accessible and intuitive, from cultural contexts like parlor games to structured conventions in source code licensing — presenting peer learning as natural and easy to adopt.

### La Realtà

Framing peer learning as "just like a parlor game" is how you get groups of people playing parlor games when they should be making decisions.

GitLab's stewardship handbook and OpenStreetMap's Data Working Group show that explicit governance documentation — not accessible framing — creates effective adoption conditions. Drupal's governance transition and Lido DAO's governance centralization demonstrate that surface accessibility without structural depth produces the failure modes the vault records: knowledge-hoarding, decision-stagnation, authority-attrition.

The wrapper that presents peer learning as natural and easy produces groups that feel smart identifying patterns and never practice them — because practice requires confronting the structural complexity the wrapper hides.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if framing peer learning as 'just like a parlor game' is how you get groups playing parlor games when they should be making decisions — and a wrapper that hides structural complexity produces groups that feel smart without ever practicing?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**about.gitlab.com** — Explicit documentation of governance boundaries reduces friction. GitLab shows that governance documentation creates more effective adoption conditions than accessible framing.

**wiki.osmfoundation.org** — Formal dispute resolution bodies resolve peer production conflicts. OpenStreetMap demonstrates that structural depth precedes effective adoption.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**drupal.org** — Intentional governance transition required years of work. Drupal shows that surface accessibility without structural depth produces governance failures.

**lido.fi** — Lido DAO governance centralization demonstrates that blockchain-based peer governance centralizes when surface framing hides structural complexity.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Il wrapper funziona quando la complessità strutturale è resa esplicita insieme all'accessibilità superficiale. GitLab e OpenStreetMap mostrano che la documentazione di governance crea condizioni di adozione efficaci.

**Regola Negativa:** Non presentare il peer learning come naturale o facile. Drupal e Lido mostrano che l'accessibilità superficiale senza profondità strutturale produce fallimenti di governance.
