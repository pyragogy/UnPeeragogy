### Il Dogma

Groups fall into stasis when decision-making stagnates, leading to lack of production and change. External stimuli are needed to break stasis. A group in stasis may disappear without being noticed due to authority attrition.

### La Realtà

Stasis isn't a coma. It's a governance design bug masquerading as a natural phenomenon.

OpenStreetMap's Data Working Group breaks decision paralysis through binding dispute resolution — not waiting for external stimulus. GitLab's stewardship handbook prevents stasis by documenting who decides when consensus fails. The Linux kernel's Code of Conflict shows that external pressure can force governance changes that create new friction patterns rather than resolving old ones. WebAssembly's lighter governance model demonstrates that stasis is the default when authority is deliberately distributed.

The pattern assumes stasis requires external crisis to resolve. The evidence shows that internal governance design — explicit authority, defined escalation, decision-making that doesn't depend on mood — prevents stasis more reliably than external shocks.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** La stasi si risolve quando diagnosticata come problema di governance design piuttosto che fase naturale del ciclo di vita del gruppo. OpenStreetMap e GitLab mostrano che autorità decisionale esplicita e escalation definita prevengono la stasi.

**Regola Negativa:** Non assumere che la stasi richieda una crisi esterna per risolversi. Linux mostra che la pressione esterna può forzare cambiamenti di governance che creano nuovi pattern di frizione.
