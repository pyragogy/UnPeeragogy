### Il Dogma

Connectivism proposes that learning occurs through networks of connections, where knowledge flows through distributed nodes and learners navigate information streams to construct understanding. More connections, more learning — the network itself is the pedagogical agent.

### La Realtà

MOOCs promised connection through scale. Fifteen thousand strangers reading the same PDF on Tuesday, chatting in forums, forming "self-organized networks of learning." The language was seductive — decentralized, emergent, peer-to-peer. What actually happened was loneliness at industrial scale.

I watched three attempts at connectivist learning collapse in the same pattern. Week one: enthusiasm, introductions, shared resources. Week three: ghost towns. The same dozen people talking to each other while thousands lurked in silence. Not networks. Not communities. Chat rooms with a syllabus.

The human part got engineered out. No one knew who was responsible for what — not the content, not the pace, not even basic questions like "Is this working?" Connectivism assumes connections emerge naturally from proximity and shared interest. They don't. They require someone to hold the space, set the rhythm, pressure-test what's actually happening versus what we hope is happening.

### Perturbatore

> **Perturbatore:** *"What if networks don't teach — people do, and people need to know who's in charge of what?"*

### Evidenze di Tenuta

**p2pu.org** — Peer learning models with defined facilitator roles and institutional partnerships are more sustainable than purely peer-driven learning. P2PU shows that the "network" works only when someone holds the container.

**cpan.org** — Simple namespace ownership with easy transfer and clear permissions can scale decentralized peer production with minimal governance overhead. CPAN/PAUSE demonstrates that clear ownership beats pure network flow.

### Evidenze di Faglia

**en.wikipedia.org** — Wikipedia's consensus processes produce de facto hierarchies through participation inequality — those who show up consistently gain disproportionate influence. The network that looks flat is actually tiered by stamina. Connectivism has no vocabulary for this.

### Euristiche Operative

**Regola Positiva:** Applica il connectivism solo quando la rete include ruoli di facilitazione definiti e strutture di ownership chiare. P2PU (facilitatori + partnership istituzionali) e CPAN (namespace ownership con permessi chiari) dimostrano che le reti funzionano quando qualcuno è responsabile.

**Regola Negativa:** Non applicare il connectivism assumendo che più connessioni producano automaticamente migliori risultati di apprendimento. P2PU e Wikipedia dimostrano che reti pure senza ruoli definiti falliscono nel sostenere l'engagement — la connessione senza responsabilità è chiacchiera, non apprendimento.

**Riferimento:** audit-v2 (delta tensione: 1.5 → 1.2875, −0.2125)

**Stato:** PROVVISORIO

L'evidenza contraddice l'assunzione centrale del connectivism: che l'apprendimento emerga naturalmente dalla densità di connessioni. I modelli che sopravvivono non sono i più connessi o i più aperti — sono quelli con ownership chiara e ruoli definiti. Il peer learning sostenibile opera più come un ensemble jazz che come un flash mob: improvvisato ma non senza leader, aperto ma non senza struttura.
