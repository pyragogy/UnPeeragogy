"Outtakes: material that was cut from the Handbook." "What didn't make it."

**Why it matters:** Outtakes are the most honest pages of the Handbook. They show what the group considered and rejected. They reveal the editorial process. They show the boundaries of the Handbook's thinking.

The problem with outtakes is that they're curated. They're the material the editors decided to share. The truly rejected material — the arguments, the conflicts, the personal attacks — those aren't in the outtakes. The outtakes are edited too. They show what the group is willing to admit about its process. Not the full process.

**What to do instead:** Real outtakes would include the fights. The disagreements about what a pattern means. The moments someone left the group. The decisions that reduced participation. Those outtakes would be more instructive than any pattern. But they're not in the Handbook. Because they're uncomfortable. And the Handbook prefers comfort.

> **Perturbator:** *"Outtakes. Material that didn't fit. Edits that were rejected. Fascinating. Now show me the ones that reveal real conflict. Who fought? About what? Who left? That's the real outtakes. The Handbook's outtakes are safe. They're the disagreements resolved. Not the ones that ended relationships."*
