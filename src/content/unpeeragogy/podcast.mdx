The Handbook's podcast section — a list of possible episodes. Not recorded. Planned.

**Why it matters:** The podcast section is a list of episodes that were supposed to be recorded. They weren't. It's a plan, not a production. The Handbook is full of these: lists of things to do, resources to create, content to produce. Most unrealized.

Planned but unexecuted content is a symptom of the gap between theory and practice. The group plans. It doesn't execute. Plans are safe. Production is risky. A planned podcast is an idea. A recorded podcast is a fact. The Handbook has many ideas. Fewer facts.

**What to do instead:** A plan is not content. A plan is a promise. If you want content, produce content. Don't list what you could produce. Produce what you can. A single episode is worth more than a list of twenty planned ones.

> **Perturbator:** *"Your podcast section is a list of episodes that were never recorded. That's not a resource. That's a wishlist. A wishlist of content you could produce and didn't. The Handbook is full of these lists. Lists of things to do. Things that will never be done. And every list is an admission: we plan more than we execute."*
