## Summary

Groups die from endless discussion when nobody can end the discussion. The pattern identifies paralysis, not productivity — talking becomes the work instead of fuel for work. Authority to decide matters more than quality of feedback.

## Euristiche Operative

### Positive Rule

> This pattern accurately identifies dysfunction when groups lack both escalation paths and binding authority. WebAssembly Community Group governance shows that lighter models trade authority for speed, creating the exact paralysis conditions this pattern describes.

### Negative Rule

> Do not apply this pattern to groups with formal escalation mechanisms or binding decision authority. IETF working groups and OpenStreetMap's Data Working Group show that extensive reflection can be productive when institutional boundaries exist to terminate discussion and enforce decisions.

## Sintesi Obliqua

The pattern claims reflection without resolution kills peer production. Partially verified — but only when groups lack escalation paths. OpenStreetMap's Data Working Group shows extensive discussion works fine with binding authority, while WebAssembly Community Groups trade decision power for speed and hit exactly this paralysis. The real mechanism isn't navel gazing itself but institutional boundaries that terminate discussion. The father's double bind works because the son has no appeals court.
